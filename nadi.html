Method & limits

NadiSense computes pulse-transit dynamics from fingertip video (reflectance PPG) and derives 13 heart-rate-variability features (SDNN, RMSSD, pNN50, SD1/SD2, LF/HF, sample entropy, turning-point ratio, ectopy-like beat fraction, spectral entropy, irregularity %). A 3-layer network trained on HRV patterns returns P(irregular rhythm). This is a SCREENING aid, not a diagnosis — irregularity has many causes (stress, caffeine, ectopic beats), and a negative result does not rule out heart disease.
